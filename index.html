SSRN / Elsevier • Under Review Registry ID: SSRN-MED-2026-ROC Peer-Reviewed Clinical Submission

Explainable Clinical Diagnostic Framework for Myocardial Pathology via Electrocardiographic Waveform Modeling & Constrained LLM Evidence Synthesis

Authorship: Co-Author (3rd Author) Domain: Biomedical Signal Processing • Explainable AI (XAI) Validation Status: Empirical Validation Complete

This research introduces a robust, dual-stage diagnostic architecture designed to bridge the gap between black-box deep learning inference and clinical utility in cardiovascular pathology. The framework extracts multiscale temporal features from standardized 12-lead ECG signals, performs gradient-weighted explainability mapping (SHAP), and constraints clinical LLMs to generate non-hallucinatory diagnostic rationales mapped directly to anatomical leads.
